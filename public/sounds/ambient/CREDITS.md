# Ambient sound credits

All recordings are CC0 (public domain) — no attribution is legally
required, but it's good practice to credit the original creators anyway.

- **rain.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
- **waves.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Sea and river wave sounds" by
  RandomMind (recorded on the Vistula river), CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds
- **lofi.mp3** — re-encoded from "Lofi Hip Hop Loop" by omfgdude, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/lofi-hip-hop-loop
- **thunder.mp3** — re-encoded from "Rain + Long Thunder" by WuxiaScrub, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-long-thunder
- **fireplace.mp3** — re-encoded from "Fireplace Sound loop" by PagDev, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop
- **night.mp3** — re-encoded from "Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable" by
  Wolfgang_, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable
- **forest.mp3** — re-encoded from "Birds and Wind - Ambient, Birds, Wind
  and Synth" (ambience-only mix) by Spring Spring, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/birds-and-wind-ambient-birds-wind-and-synth
- **coffeeshop.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Coffee Shop Ambience,
  Northtown Coffee, Arcata, CA" by CVLTIV8R (freesound.org), CC0.
  https://freesound.org/people/CVLTIV8R/sounds/813868/

rain.mp3/waves.mp3/thunder.mp3/fireplace.mp3/night.mp3/coffeeshop.mp3 were
trimmed to a short loop segment (~25-50s depending on the source); lofi.mp3
and forest.mp3 are kept at their full original loop length (~2:08 and
~1:24) since trimming either would cut the music/ambience mid-phrase. All
were re-encoded to MP3 at 128kbps to keep file size reasonable for a web
app; the originals are longer/higher bitrate.
