# Ambient sound credits

All three recordings are CC0 (public domain) — no attribution is legally
required, but it's good practice to credit the original creators anyway.

- **rain.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
- **waves.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Sea and river wave sounds" by
  RandomMind (recorded on the Vistula river), CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds
- **lofi.mp3** — re-encoded from "Lofi Hip Hop Loop" by omfgdude, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/lofi-hip-hop-loop

rain.mp3/waves.mp3 were trimmed to a ~45s loop segment; lofi.mp3 is the
full ~2:08 loop (trimming it would cut the music mid-phrase). All three
were re-encoded to MP3 at 128kbps to keep file size reasonable for a web
app; the originals are longer/higher bitrate.
