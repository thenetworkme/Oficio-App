# Ambient sound credits

Both recordings are CC0 (public domain) — no attribution is legally required,
but it's good practice to credit the original creators anyway.

- **rain.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
- **waves.mp3** — trimmed/re-encoded from "Sea and river wave sounds" by
  RandomMind (recorded on the Vistula river), CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds

Both were re-encoded (MP3, 128kbps, trimmed to a ~45s loop segment) to keep
file size reasonable for a web app; the originals are longer/higher bitrate.

"Lofi" has no recording — it's still synthesized in `lib/sound/ambient.ts`
(not flagged as a problem, and a cleanly-licensed lofi loop is much harder
to source than ambient noise).
